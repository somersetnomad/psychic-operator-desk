# Psychic Line Operator Desk

A single-file, offline web console for psychic line operators: a full 78-card tarot reading desk with a live horary astrology chart cast at the moment each reading is drawn.

Open `index.html` in any modern browser — no install, no server, no internet connection needed.

## Tarot desk

- Hand-written upright and reversed meanings for all 78 cards
- Seven spreads: 3-Card Emotional Mirror, Single Card, Past–Present–Future, Situation–Obstacle–Advice, Relationship 5-Card, 5-Card Cross, Celtic Cross
- Verbatim scripts personalised with the caller's name, or concise operator bullets
- "Ask" follow-up question for every card
- Per-card redraw / flip / copy, clarifier cards, and a physical-deck mode for picking the cards you actually drew
- Spread insights: Major Arcana weight, suit/element balance, reversals, court cards, repeating numbers
- Eight caller situations that reshape every script
- Closing reassurance phrases plus extra phrases per situation
- Call timer with configurable length and Opening → Reading → Wrap-up → Close prompts
- Reading history saved in the browser, reloadable and exportable as .txt
- "Caller in distress" safeguarding panel with what to say and UK support lines
- Keyboard shortcuts (press `?`)

## Horary chart

Cast automatically for the exact moment a new reading is drawn (time and location are editable).

- Tropical zodiac, **Regiomontanus** houses
- **Traditional 7 planets** carry essential dignity and judgement: domicile, exaltation, triplicity (Dorothean), **terms (Egyptian)**, **faces / decans (Chaldean)**, detriment, fall, peregrine — scored on Lilly's scale
- **Uranus and Pluto** shown on the wheel and in the table for reference (toggleable), excluded from dignity and judgement
- Retrograde motion, combust / cazimi / under the beams
- Radicality: planetary day and hour, early/late Ascendant, Moon void of course, Via Combusta, Saturn in the 1st or 7th
- Moon phase, last aspect, upcoming perfections with times, sign change
- Significators for the caller and the quesited house chosen by the caller's situation, with applying/separating aspect analysis
- Ptolemaic aspects with orbs by moiety, Part of Fortune, full cusp list
- Plain-language "say it simply" script for the caller

Planetary positions come from [astronomy-engine](https://github.com/cosinekitty/astronomy) (MIT License, © Don Cross), embedded in the page.

## Note

Readings are for guidance and entertainment. The safeguarding panel lists UK services; check them against your line's own escalation procedure.
